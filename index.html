WAS / WERE

Past of the verb to be · Read first, then decide.

1 · Which one fits the story?

Think about who and when. Tap was or were.

2 · Spot the mistake

Is the sentence correct or wrong?

3 · Build the question

Tap the words in order. Then answer with a short answer.

4 · True for you?

Decide if each sentence is true for you. If not, make it true!

Now talk: Ask your teacher (or a friend) 3 questions: Were you at…? Was your…? Answer with Yes, I was / No, I wasn't.
